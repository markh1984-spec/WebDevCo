About

Rory Joscelyne

Rory is a filmmaker and photographer at Cyberpunk Studios, a small independent film studio in South Essex.

He wrote Turning Tide, which won the Raindance competition in 2021, and wrote and directed Human Cargo, nominated for awards in 2019. The stills come from the same habits as the films: wait for the light, find the frame, and let the space do the talking.
