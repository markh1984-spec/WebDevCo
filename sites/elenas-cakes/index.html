About Elena

It started with one birthday cake.

I baked a cake for my daughter's birthday, and someone at the party asked if I'd make one for theirs. I've been baking them ever since, and I still get the same nervous excitement every time I hand one over.

I bake everything from scratch in small batches — no premade mixes, no fondant-covered cardboard. The rainbow sponge is coloured layer by layer and stacked by hand, which is why the inside gets as much attention as the outside. If a cake leaves my kitchen, it's because I'd happily serve it at my own table.

  • Made fresh to order, never frozen
  • Free-from options: gluten-free, dairy-free, egg-free
  • Local delivery, or collect from me
  • Fully registered & food-hygiene rated
